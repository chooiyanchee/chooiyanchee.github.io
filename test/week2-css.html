My Favourite Food

Char Kuey Teow

My favourite food is Char Kuey Teow. It is a popular noodle dish in Malaysia and Singapore, made with flat rice noodles stir-fried with shrimp, Chinese sausage, eggs, bean sprouts, and chives. The dish is known for its smoky flavor and is often served with a side of lime and chili sauce.
