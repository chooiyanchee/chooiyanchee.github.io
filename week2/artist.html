My Favourite Artist

brb.

brb. is a Singaporean R&B and pop music group formed in 2018. The band consists of three members: Marc Lian,Clarence Liew, and Auzaie Zie. The name brb. represents the group's relaxed and casual approach to music,reflecting their smooth sounds and laid-back style. Their music combines elements of R&B, pop, and electronicmusic, creating a unique sound that appeals to young listeners.
The group gained attention through songs such as Cool With It, Relationshit and Talking to the Moon. Their music often explores themes of love, relationships, heartbreak, and personal experiences. With catchy melodies, smooth vocals, and modern production, brb. has built a growing fan base in Singapore and internationally.
brb. is also known for collaborating with other artist and experimenting with different musical styles. Their creative approach has helped them stand out in the Asian music scene. Through their relatable lyrics and distinctive sound, brb. continues to connec with listeners who enjoy contemporary R&B and pop music. Overall, brb. is a talented and promising music group that has made a mark in the Singaporean music industry and continues to captivate audiences with their music.
